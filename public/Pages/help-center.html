FAQs
How do I become a writer?
Go to Profile → Edit Profile → enable Writer mode. Or tap “Become a Writer” from Home.
How do I change my theme?
Open Settings → Appearance → pick Light or Dark. It applies everywhere instantly.
How do I manage my library?
Use Library to track reading, saved stories, and history. Clear history from Library → History → Clear.
How do I contact support?
Tap Contact Support below or email support@droboard.app. We reply within 24 hours.
