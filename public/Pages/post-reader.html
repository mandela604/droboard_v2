Career Story
My boss promoted the intern I trained over me. Here's what I did next.
Three years building that department from scratch. He came in six months ago. I trained him. I answered his questions at midnight. I gave him my contacts. Then they announced his promotion in the all-hands. My name wasn't mentioned once. I didn't quit. I documented everything and sent it to HR. Then I got a job offer from a competitor paying 60% more. Sometimes the best revenge is just leaving with your dignity and a better salary.
